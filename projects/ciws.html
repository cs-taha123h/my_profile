CIWS simulation

About

Worked on developing a CWIS simulation that simulated drag, gravity and other metrics. The simulation was coded in C++ and use openframeworks as a bases for the projects. Users have the option to control the system and shoot down incoming missiles to protect a simulated ship, it is more difficult than imagined as bullets do not move instanly and you are therefore required to aim infront on the path of the missile to successfully intercept them.

Overall this project was for my games computing module and I was able to achieve a 92% grade with no feedback on improvement as the simulation was clear and users could easily understand the objectives, and see how well they were doing compared to other users.
